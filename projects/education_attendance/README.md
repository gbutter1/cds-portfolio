# K-12 Daily Attendance & Chronic Absenteeism

**Sector:** Education (K-12 district)
**Question answered:** Which schools and grades have the highest chronic
absenteeism, how does attendance move through the year, and how much of the
source extract can be trusted?

## Source data

Generated by `generate.py` from a fixed seed. 11 schools across three
clusters, 2,400 students, one school year (Aug 2025 – May 2026), ~427,000
daily attendance rows. Realistic structure: seasonality (flu season, end of
year), day-of-week effects, higher absence propensity at Title I schools, a
long tail of chronically absent students, and ~6% mid-year transfers.

Deliberate defects: duplicate rows, `elm01 `-style code casing, invalid status
codes, holiday-dated rows, orphan student IDs, and attendance posted after a
student withdrew. Every one is caught in staging and reported on the
dashboard's data-quality page.

## Pipeline

1. `python projects/education_attendance/generate.py` → `data/raw/education_attendance/*.csv`
2. `python pipeline/load_raw.py education_attendance` → `raw_education_attendance.*` (all text)
3. `python pipeline/run_dbt.py build` → `staging.stg_*` views, `marts.*` tables, 36 tests
4. `python pipeline/run_evidence.py sources && ... build` → dashboard pages under `evidence/pages/education-attendance/`

## Model

| Table | Grain |
|---|---|
| `marts.dim_school` | school |
| `marts.fct_attendance_daily` | student × school day (valid rows only) |
| `marts.student_attendance_summary` | student (year-to-date totals, rates, absence tier, chronic flag) |
| `marts.school_monthly_attendance` | school × month |
| `marts.attendance_data_quality` | dq_status |

Definitions (attendance rate, chronic absenteeism, enrolled day) are on the
dashboard's "Definitions" panel and in the model SQL.

## Modeled on

Attendance, enrollment and school-dimension work delivered for a large
metro-Atlanta school district (SQL Server / Snowflake). Structure and
patterns are reproduced here on synthetic data; no district data is used.
